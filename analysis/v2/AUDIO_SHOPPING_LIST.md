# Audio to upload (licensed for YouTube monetization)

Drop the files into the chat as WAV (preferred) or high-bitrate MP3. A zip is fine. Name each file after what it is (e.g. `whoosh_big_1.wav`). Every bed sits about 24 dB under the voice, as in the reference, so beds with a strong lead melody won't work. Pick pulse-driven tracks.

## Music beds

The reference uses dark, low-register hip-hop/trap library beds at about 100 BPM, with a different track per entry.

| # | Use | Feel | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intro + recap | dark trap/hip-hop, 95–105 BPM, steady pulse, minimal melody | 2+ min |
| 2 | Harald Hardrada (Vikings) | dark and epic: low drums, Nordic or cinematic colour | 4+ min |
| 3 | Attila the Hun | ominous, brooding, war-drum feel | 3+ min |
| 4 | Crassus (Rome) | tense / sneaky-dark | 3+ min |
| 5 | Arrichion (Olympics) | tense build to epic | 3+ min |
| 6 | Yi Sun-sin (naval battle) | epic, emotional build | 4+ min |
| 7 | Mid-roll pitch | light lo-fi | 1 min |
| 8 | Outro sting | punchy 105–110 BPM beat | 6–10 s |

If you can only get a few, three tracks are enough: one dark trap bed, one epic/tense bed and one lo-fi bed. I'll cut and reuse them per entry.

## Sound effects (recorded, not synthesized)

**Editing kit (the most important)**
- 1–2 cinematic hits/booms for title cards (bright attack, about 1 s tail).
- 3–4 whooshes (short swish to big swoosh).
- 2–3 UI pops/clicks for text and labels.
- 2–3 impacts/thuds, plus 1 punch.
- 1–2 risers (1–2 s) and 1 reverse cymbal.
- 1 record scratch.

**Story effects**
- Weapons: sword clash, sword unsheath, arrow whoosh, arrow impact.
- Destruction: wood door crash, metal clang/chains, fire whoosh, fire crackle loop.
- Naval and battle: cannon, musket/arquebus shot, splash, ship/wood crash, war drum hits (taiko-style).
- Crowds: cheer, gasp, applause.
- Comedy: crickets, heartbeat, snore, coin/cash register, sad trombone (optional).
- Bells: funeral toll, boxing bell, notification ding.

**Ambience loops (30 s+)**
- Battlefield.
- River with birds.
- Sea waves.
- Wind.
- Crowd murmur.
- Fire crackle.

Anything you can't find, I'll design myself and list in the changelog.

# Part 07 summary (frames 9870-11279)

- Hard cuts dominate: every scene change in this range is a hard cut (f9874, 9920, 9962, 10012, 10075, 10159, 10221, 10506, 10609, 10676, 10746, 10823, 11012, 11164). No dissolves, wipes or flashes between normal shots.
- Fade-to-black is used only around the chapter card: book fades out f10303-10309, black f10309-10315, card fades in f10316-10325, holds to f10349, fades out f10350-10357, next shot fades up from black f10358-10362.
- Chapter card (f10316-10357): black, gold revolver-cylinder emblem, spaced gold "CHAPTER VI", cream blackletter "A Good Man". Pure alpha fades, no scale or slide.
- Name tags (white rounded tag, bold caps + small lowercase sub-line) always pop from a tiny tilted sliver (~4% wide) and grow with a tilt over ~5-6 frames, then level out: THE DOCTOR f9886-9892, EDITH & ARCHIE / widow and son f10536-10542, RAINS FALL / native chief f10711-10717, SISTER CALDERON / once helped f11060-11066. They exit by hard cut, except SISTER CALDERON which fades out f11119-11121.
- "EARLY RELEASE" (f10789-10795) is a plain white caps label with dark outline and no tag box, but it uses the same tiny-to-full tilted pop.
- Red stamps (TUBERCULOSIS f9926-9928, NO CURE f10095-10098, EVICTED f10657-10660) slam in with a faint oversized first frame (~1.3x), then a near-opaque frame, then settle at 1.0 with a slight tilt in ~2-3 frames. NO CURE fades out f10111-10113; the others leave on a cut.
- HONOR HUD (dark bar, label "HONOR", LOW/HIGH, minus icon left, gold coin right, red-to-white gradient with pointer, top-left) pops in from a tiny sliver in ~3-4 frames (f10401-10404, f10567-10570) and fades out over ~3 frames (f10501-10503, f10670-10673). It survives hard cuts between shots while on.
- Handwriting/drawing overlays draw on in sequence: "What kind of man?" is written letter by letter with a pencil icon (f10254-10277), ? drawn stroke by stroke (f10271-10277), red circle on the photo (f10043-10049), white curved arrow (f10052-10061), red health-graph line (f10135-10144), red underline then yellow highlight on "good man" (f10960-10975).
- Small props (wanted-poster icon, banknote stack, photo, HEALTH note, gold ring) enter by scale-pop from tiny to full in ~3-4 frames.
- Storybook shot (f10221-10308): open book with Arthur sketch on left page; elements are added to the right page in order (wanted poster f10239, money f10245, handwriting f10254, photo f10259); slight continuous book drift/tilt; embers in the background.
- Letter sequence (f10823-11011): top-down desk with envelope sliding in, wax seal cracking f10865, flap opening f10866-10870, letter sliding out f10871-10878, ring dropping f10893-10902, sparkle f10905-10923, then cut to Arthur reading the letter "Dear Arthur, / There's a good man within you. / Mary".
- Camera habit: mostly static art with a slow, subtle push-in or drift on almost every shot (sunset cliff, crossroads, depot, close-ups, desk shots, medallion). The only abrupt scale change is a punch-in cut within the mining-camp scene at f10564 (~1.35x).
- Character animation is idle only (breathing bob, blinks, head sway) plus a few gestures: Arthur's stop-hand then cough (f9990-10011), hat-touch pose (f10515-10563, f10686-10745), pouch handoff (f10573-10595), suitcase throw (f10621-10630), nun's hand on shoulder (f11223-11228).
- FX: cough smoke puffs (f9993-10011), hoof dust (f10452-10500), lightning bolt with sky flash (f10367-10373), sun rays/glow in the sunset shots, sparkle on money pouch (f10596-10607), door dust (f10762-10781), papers burst (f10631-10675), pink bokeh in the final close-up (f11164+), floating dust/ember specks in many shots.
- Dark vignette treatments: opening shot (f9870-9873), diagnosis desk, sepia photo, medallion wall, storybook, desk/letter shots.
- Sepia old photograph (f9962-10011) shows the characters in a gold-cornered frame; it returns hanging on the wall in the next close-up (f10012-10074), then a small copy is pasted into the storybook.
- Shot rhythm: typical shots are 40-100 frames; the longest holds are the nun close-up (>115 frames, still running at f11279) and the Arthur-with-letter shot (f10924-11011, 88 frames).
- The Honor meter pointer position/stars could not be read reliably on the sheets; only the full-res f10600 was inspected.
- Text I could not read with certainty: the year at top-right of the storybook right page ("1899" is a guess), the wanted-poster text (read as WANTED) and the "THAT COUGH" plaque (tiny, read as "THAT COUGH"). The "LOW ROAD" / "HIGH ROAD" signpost labels are read from thumbnails only.
- The range ends mid-shot: the nun/Arthur close-up (from f11164) is still running at f11279; the sunset horseback shot (f10433) ends at the cut f10506 so its f1 is 10505.

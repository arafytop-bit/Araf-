# Part 03 summary (frames 4230-5639)

- Cuts are almost always HARD cuts on a new shot (4236, 4308, 4535, 4594, 4722, 4776, 4812, 4921, 4996, 5027, 5161, 5228, 5301, 5384, 5445, 5489, 5557). Roughly one cut every 3-4 s; labels/arrows never survive a cut.
- Fades are reserved for section boundaries: campfire memory -> fade to black f4427-4433, black gap, chapter card fade in f4441-4449 / out f4472-4482, fade up into steamboat f4483-4490; a fade-to-white (radial, grey corners) f5629-5636 into the "TWO DAYS LATER" scene.
- Chapter card "CHAPTER I / Blackwater": black screen, small gold wagon-wheel emblem, tiny gold spaced "CHAPTER I", off-white blackletter "Blackwater" (~24% wide, centred). Pure opacity fade, no motion, held ~1 s fully visible (f4449-4472).
- Memory/flashback bubble (f4385-4426): cream oval glow dissolves in over left half of a campfire shot showing a boy in suspenders by a ranch; fades out with the whole shot.
- White label style (LAWMEN, NOT ON THE BOAT, THE LAW, ABANDONED, HEATING: 0, SCARIER, you, the boat, $150,000, JOHN?): bold caps (small lowercase for "you"/"the boat"), white fill, thick black outline, NO tag background. Pattern: appears as a tiny rotated (~-8deg) dot, scales up over ~3-4 frames with slight overshoot, settles ~5 frames after first sight at ~20% x / ~14-24% y.
- Every such label is followed by a hand-drawn white curved ARROW that draws on stroke-wise over ~8-12 frames (starts ~4-5 frames after label settles), pointing at the referent (man, boat, stove, wagon, cabin roof, Arthur). Arrows persist until the cut.
- White name tags (MICAH BELL / new guy f4556-4593; SADIE ADLER / widow f5462-5488) are rounded white tags with bold caps + tiny grey italic sub-line; they pop from a tiny dot with rotation (~-8deg -> -3deg), scale up over ~4 frames, then hold. Exit is a hard cut with the shot.
- Stamps: red distressed rubber stamps MASSACRE (f4895, 65%,28%) and HIDDEN (f5084, 62%,27%) animate identically: 1 faint frame, 1 oversized (~1.5-1.6x) frame, 2-3 frames shrinking, settled by ~+3 frames, slight tilt ~-4 to -6deg. HIDDEN later ghosts to ~40% opacity (f5109-5112) while the camera tilts. "TWO DAYS LATER" (f5637-5639) is the same stamp behaviour but BLUE/navy with rounded border.
- Persistent corner tag "RED DEAD REDEMPTION 2 · 1899": dark tag with white serif caps at top-right (83%,7%), fades in f4506-4512, survives two hard cuts (4535, 4594) as an overlay layer, fades out f4635-4639. A white "1899" tag pops at lower-left f4498-4500 over the boat shot and is gone at the f4535 cut.
- Map shot (f4594-5160) is a recurring prop: red marker ellipse draws on f4613-4620, red X f4623-4626, "BLACKWATER" types on letter-by-letter f4693-4706, money-bag icon drops onto buildings f5037-5044, red "?" f5047-5049, HIDDEN stamp. Camera on the map is a series of zoom/pan moves with ease-out (f4609-4620, f4672-4692, f5027-5043, f5109-5112) separated by static holds.
- Camera habit: most flat painted shots are essentially static or have a very slow push-in/drift (campfire, steamboat, horse in snow, tent). Deliberate moves are eased zooms/pans on the map and a quick pull-out on the snow village (f5346-5358) and a push-in on the camp (f4578-4593).
- Characters are mostly idle loops (breathing bob, waving, horse leg cycle, gallop with white speed lines f5161-5227). Notable one-offs: lawman silhouettes pop in one by one (f4831, 4838, 4844), Arthur tipping his hat (f5454-5488), Arthur turns red/sweating (f5532+ with blue sweat drop from 5521).
- FX: campfire flame flicker + sparks (4368-4426); steam breath puffs cycling on shivering Arthur (5384-5444) with white shiver arcs; gunshot starburst icons on the steamboat (f4891-4919) and growing dark smoke; full-frame cream gunshot flash f4937-4943 with a white hat that spins and falls (f4942-4979); falling cash bills pile (f4791-4811); light snow and mist (5228-5300).
- Palette-based scene logic: sunset orange/purple for Blackwater, warm brown for interior/map wall, deep navy for night chase, pale blue/white for the Colter/snow section, with dark vignettes on corners of snow shots.
- Money: "$150,000" label pops at f4793 in the cabin shot, arrow draws f4797-4804 to the cash chest; cash bills pile up f4791-4800 (green bills, per-frame pops).
- Intro of the range: Arthur-as-a-kid sequence f4236-4307 has a tiny "$10" grey text near a hat on the ground fading in f4274-4278 (very small; could not be verified at full res, exact placement approximate).
- Silhouette sequences (lawmen at dock, gunman at window wall f4921-4995, crowd in snow f5301-5383, lone man walking away f5580-5610) are used for threat/loss beats; the lone man vanishes as a ghost fade f5598-5610 while "JOHN?" label appears.
- Unreadable/uncertain: the tiny "$10" (f4274+), exact path of memory-bubble boy details, sub-line text on the "1899" tag (none), and whether the 4368 campfire reveal is a camera pan or hard cut (treated as a slide/cut). "the boat" and "you" labels are very small; read from contact sheets only.
- Transitions never use wipes, zooms-as-transitions, whips, or flashes between shots; the only flash is the diegetic gunshot flash at f4937.

# Part 06 summary (frames 8460-9869, sheets s_0282-s_0328)

- Cuts are almost exclusively hard cuts, on the first frame of a new scene (f8508, 8597, 8633, 8750, 8842, 8899, 8947, 9123, 9177, 9241, 9291, 9340, 9380, 9453, 9519, 9597, 9760). No dissolves, wipes or flashes between scenes. Shots last about 40-110 frames (1.3-3.7 s), with the longest at roughly 3.7 s.
- Fade-through-black is used only around chapter cards. Fade-out is ~10 frames, black hold 7-8 frames, card fade-in ~8 frames, hold ~25 frames, fade-out ~8 frames, black hold ~2-7 frames, fade-in to the next scene ~7 frames. Chapter IV (Guarma): out f9005-9016, black to f9022, card in f9023-9030, hold to f9055, out f9056-9063, next scene in f9065-9072. Chapter V (The Cough): out f9624-9633, black to f9640, card in f9641-9648, hold to f9674, out f9675-9682, in f9683-9689.
- Chapter card layout: black, small gold ship-wheel emblem at 50%,38%, tiny spaced gold caps "CHAPTER IV"/"CHAPTER V" at 47%, white blackletter title ("Guarma", "The Cough") at 53%. All three fade together with no movement.
- Name tags/labels (text-only labels, not full tags, in a few cases) all use the same entrance. They appear tiny and tilted ~-8 to -10 deg, grow and straighten over ~4-6 frames, and settle ~2 frames later. There is no overshoot pop. They leave only through the hard cut. Examples: PINKERTONS f8643-8649, HOSEA f8757-8764, LENNY f8847-8852, GUARMA f9151-9157, REVOLUTION f9223-9227, SUNBURNED f9385-9392, TIRED f9408-9414, COUGHS TODAY f9713-9720.
- Name-tag sub-lines are lowercase and small: "hired to hunt you" under PINKERTONS, "tropical island" under GUARMA, "(very)" under TIRED. HOSEA, LENNY, SUNBURNED and REVOLUTION have no readable sub-line.
- A tag that opens on an empty prop is a recurring gag: HOSEA over his empty chair and hat (f8757), LENNY over his lone flat cap (f8847). These are the "dead character" beats.
- Red rubber stamps slam in large and faint, then shrink to settle. ARRESTED: first visible f8923 (faint, ~1.3x), full f8924, settled f8927 (~0.85x, tilt ~-4 deg). LONG STORY: first visible f9245, large f9246-9247 (~1.5x), settled ~f9250. Both hold until the hard cut with a small wobble.
- White hand-drawn curved arrows with a black outline point from a label to the subject. SUNBURNED arrow draws f9390-9401, TIRED arrow f9411-9421, REVOLUTION arrow f9227-9238. The arrow draws on stroke-wise from its tail, then the arrowhead appears last.
- Red marker annotations draw on over ~8-10 frames: the circle around the bank icon on the map (f8464-8473), the dashed arrow (f8479-8486), the arrow between the polaroids (f8545-8551), the ellipse round PLANS THAT WORKED (f9598-9601), and the strike-through on list line 1 (f9560-9566). Red is the only annotation colour used on paper or wood props. White is used for outlines on sky or character backgrounds.
- Prop-on-wall devices: a dark wood-plank wall with a lantern glow and dust motes holds pinned polaroids/cards. The BANK / LAST JOB polaroid pops from a tiny dot to full size (f8511-8518). The postcard flips on its vertical axis (edge-on at f8554, with sparkles) to reveal TAHITI / PARADISE (f8554-8556). Light rays appear behind the Tahiti card from ~f8556.
- Map shot f8460-8507 opens with a fast ease-out zoom and rotation settle (f8460-8472) onto the SAINT DENIS parchment, then holds still.
- THINGS TO REMEMBER list (f9519-9596): list is "1. DUTCH ALWAYS HAS A PLAN / 2. WE'RE FAMILY / 3. JOHN = IDIOT", with a Dutch sketch and a horse sketch. Line 1 gets a red strike (f9560-9566), then red handwriting "DOES HE?" types on letter by letter f9571-9580 (D, DO, DOE, DOES, DOES H, DOES HE, DOES HE?). The camera pushes in and drifts across the page.
- DUTCH'S PLANS sign (f9597-9629): "DUTCH'S PLANS" over 5 chalk tally strokes, "PLANS THAT WORKED: 0" with a red "0" and a red ellipse drawn around it. Dutch stands at right with a raised hand. The camera pushes in and drifts right, then it fades to black f9624-9633 into the Chapter V card.
- Counter: "COUGHS TODAY" tag with a yellow/gold bold numeral that pops in at 41 (f9716), then ticks slowly 42 (f9724), 43 (f9730), 44 (f9745). It then accelerates: 46 f9747, 47 f9748, 50 f9749, 53 f9750, 56 f9751, 61 f9752, 66 f9753, 73 f9754, 81 f9755, 89 f9756, 100 f9757, and holds at 109 from f9758. This is ease-in acceleration, one value per frame at the end.
- Camera habit: a slow continuous push-in on almost every shot (about 8-15% over the shot), often with a slight drift. There are no shakes except a micro shake during the fort explosion (~f9304-9312). There are two exceptions to the smooth drift: a parallax pan (tram slides left, f9760-9805) and a snap punch-in at f9806 on the same street background (cut-like).
- FX vocabulary: rain streaks (Pinkerton night, storm at sea, rooftop), lightning bolt with a 2-3 frame sky flash (f9100-9102), spotlight dust, campfire flicker with floating embers, fireflies, a cartoon explosion starburst with black smoke puffs and a shockwave ring (f9304-9339, peak f9318-9324), cream starburst flash over the money bag (f8625-8632), cough arcs "((( )))" around the head (f9793-9805), dust puff at the knee (f9822-9845), sparkles around the Tahiti card.
- Character animation is minimal: idle sway and breathing, an arms-crossed to hands-at-belt pose swap (Arthur, f9486-9487), a cough cycle (f9711 onward), a hard pose swap to kneeling at f9819, and a hand reaching in from the right edge f9846-9858. Faces rarely lip-sync. Group characters are static.
- Pacing of scenes in this range: money bag trap, Pinkertons (Pinkerton boss and 5 agents with rooftop silhouettes), Hosea's empty chair, Lenny's cap, ARRESTED lawmen, harbour night, Chapter IV Guarma (ship storm and sinking, beach with Arthur, fort/revolution, LONG STORY, explosion, sailboat, sunburned/tired close-up), camp two-shot, list, sign, Chapter V The Cough (camp coughing, 109 counter, street, kneeling with offered hand).
- Colour grading shifts hard at cuts: dark blue rain night to dusk purple to bright tropical day to red-orange sunset to dark night camp to grey-brown street. The cut at f9123 goes from a dark storm directly to a bright beach with no transition.
- One-off: the ship breaks apart (sails fall off, hull sinks) f9096-9122 under a lightning flash, a dramatic mid-scene cut at f9123.
- One-off: the vignette and background blur ramp at f9855-9869 (street bystanders blur out while Arthur stays sharp). It probably leads into a transition just after this range; it was not verifiable because the range ends at f9869.
- Unreadable or approximate text: tiny "BANK" on the polaroid building and its "LAST JOB" caption were read at thumbnail size. Sub-lines for HOSEA, LENNY, REVOLUTION and SUNBURNED were not resolved (none visible). The ST. DENIS tram text is illegible. No full-res check was done on the counter label ("COUGHS TODAY" read from the thumbnail only).

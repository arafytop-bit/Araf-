# Part 08 summary (frames 11280-12689)

- Chapter card (f11317-11357): black screen, tiny gold ship-wheel emblem + spaced gold "CHAPTER VII" + white blackletter "The Rat". It fades in over about 5 frames (f11317-11322), holds about 25 frames, and fades out over about 10 frames (f11348-11357). There are about 8 black frames before it (f11309-11316) and 1 after (f11358). It does not scale or move. The preceding shot fades to black f11300-11309 and the next shot fades up from black f11359-11365.
- Cuts are hard cuts almost everywhere (f11436, 11480, 11556, 11635, 11759, 11835, 11979, 12082, 12126, 12190, 12330, 12374, 12404, 12498, 12598). The only dissolve-like moment is the white-haze pose swap at f11687-11691 (John removes his hat). The only fades are the chapter-card in/out.
- Shot length is about 2-5 s on average. Longer holds (4-5 s) carry bubbles and name tags. Shots alternate between painted flat-character scenes and prop/paper close-ups (portrait, notebook, map, plan sketch).
- Camera: nearly every shot gets a very slow continuous push-in (about 3-10% over the shot): portrait f11359-11435, two-shot f11480-11555, Dutch/Hosea f11759-11834 and f12082-12125, Milton interior f12190-12329. Paper props get larger moves: notebook push+drift up f11835-11911, then a punch-in hard zoom at f11912, then drift to f11978; plan paper swaying/tilting plus zoom to the right-hand car f12527-12546, pulling back f12560-12570. One fast "zoom-on-reveal" f12374-12377 (wide to chest-up on Hosea) after the silhouette cut. The rider and sunset shots (f12126-12189, f11635-11758) are essentially static.
- Name tags (white rounded rectangle, bold black caps + small sub-line): ABIGAIL / John's girl (f12028), SADIE ADLER / the widow (f12151), AGENT MILTON / Pinkerton (f12194), SINCE GUARMA / (the island) (f12421). All start as a tiny tilted sliver, pop to about 1.1x, and settle to 1.0 and 0deg within about 4-6 frames. They stay until the shot cuts out and do not fade out. Milton's tag enters from the right with a tilt.
- Speech bubbles (white rounded, bold black text, tail toward speaker): "Dutch..." (f11437-11456), "John didn't make it." (f11770-11834), "Leave her." (f12101-12125). Pop from tiny to full over about 4-5 frames (slight overshoot). The "Dutch..." bubble fades/shrinks out (f11455-11457); the others leave with the hard cut.
- Red rubber stamps: RAT on Hosea (f12378, ghost larger at f12378, red slam f12379, shrinks to settle by about f12381) and MICAH'S PLAN (f12577 ghost, f12578 slam, settled f12582). The same pattern: oversized and translucent for 1 frame, slam at about 1.3x, ease down to 1.0. Both tilt about -4 to -8deg and stay until the cut.
- Counter "LEFT BEHIND n" (top-left white slanted tag with a black number box, gold "1" then orange-red "2"): it appears f11610 (tiny, full by f11616), persists across a hard cut at f11635, and increments 1 to 2 at f11733-11737 with a flicker and orange glow. It disappears at the cut f11759.
- THINGS TO REMEMBER list (f11835-11978): page now reads 1. DUTCH ALWAYS HAS A PLAN (struck) DOES HE? (red), 2. WE'RE FAMILY, 3. JOHN = IDIOT, with a Dutch sketch and a horse doodle. A pencil slides in and underlines item 2 (f11886-11911). A red "?" pops in at the right of item 2 (f11935-11938). The list is already struck-through on first appearance, so there is no strike animation in this range.
- Hand-drawn annotations: red circles with a centre dot and a red serif label appear in sequence on the map (CAMP f12451, NEXT JOB f12463, NAMES f12470). They are faint and small at first, full within about 2 frames, with slight overshoot. A red circle on the plan paper is drawn stroke by stroke (f12549-12559). Labels type on letter by letter ("left behind" f12555-12566; "— Micah" f12562-12574). JOHN and the stick figure fade in.
- Arrow: "NEW FAVORITE" (f11519-11555) is white handwriting-style caps that scale in (f11519-11524), followed by a curved white arrow that draws on (f11523-11537) and points to the blond man (Hosea).
- Question mark devices: golden "?" over the silhouette man (f12334-12372) pops from tiny to full over about 2 frames and fades out over about 3 frames. A red "?" on the notebook uses the same pop.
- Characters are flat, outlined, and rigged: idle breathing sway, blink, head bob when speaking, small sound-wave arcs near the face (f12636-12689 for Dutch, f11485-11530 for Hosea). Poses can swap abruptly (Milton arm snaps out holding a revolver at f12229, Dutch's arm raise at f11446, John's hat removal f11687-11691). Riders on horses use an in-place gallop loop with dust puffs on a static background (f12126-12189).
- Prop animations: envelopes with red wax seal "M." fly in an arc from the left edge to a pile every 13-14 frames (f12406-12497) and floating envelopes flutter between Milton and the silhouette (f12330-12373). A bullet-impact dot with spider cracks appears on the glass over Micah's portrait at f11397-11399 (cracks burst over 2 frames) and then stays.

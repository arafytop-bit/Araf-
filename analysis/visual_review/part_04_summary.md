# Part 04 summary (frames 5640-7049)

- **Hard cuts dominate**: ~19 hard cuts in 47 s with no dissolve, whip or wipe between scenes (e.g. 5697, 5740, 5805, 5896, 5964, 6225, 6319, 6388, 6472, 6562, 6630, 6936, 6987, 7016). Shot lengths run from ~25 frames to ~3 s; the ledger, Strauss and Pearson shots are 50-95 frames.
- **Fade to/from black** is used only around chapter changes: scene fade-out f6049-6057, ~8 frames of black, chapter card fades in f6065-6072, holds ~25 frames, fades out f6097-6106, next scene fades in from black f6107-6114.

- **Name tags** ("PEARSON / camp cook" f6236, "STRAUSS / loan shark" f6958, "THOMAS DOWNES / sick farmer" f7021): white rounded rectangle with dark outline, bold caps, small sub-line. They always appear ~1-2 s after the shot starts, pop from a speck, tilted ~-8deg, scale up with ~1.1 overshoot over ~4 frames (e.g. 6958 -> 6963) and then level out. They exit by hard cut with the shot.
- **Single-word labels** ("UNCLE" f6324, "VALENTINE" f6190): same tag pop in. "LUMBAGO / (allegedly)" (f6356) is plain white outlined caps with no box.

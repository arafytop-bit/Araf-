# Trust Me Bro thumbnail format: analysis (7 references)

References: CRAZY PLACES, WRONG FACTS, RABBIT HOLES, DARK PAST, UNKNOWN, ANCIENT MYSTERIES, LOST HISTORY
(plus the partially visible DREAM MEANINGS and WORST DEALS).

Measurements were taken on 960x540 crops of the phone screenshots.

## 1. Layout (identical on every thumbnail)

```
+--------------------------------------------------+
| 2% margin                                        |
|  BIG  TITLE  WORDS  (97% of width)               |  <- top band, text centre ~8-11% of H
|        ( hat / head overlaps the letters )       |
|   side         \  HUGE  HEAD  /        side      |
|  character      \  ~60% of H /       character   |
|  + prop          \__________/        + prop      |
|  (40-45% scale)   body cut by frame   (story)    |
+--------------------------------------------------+
```

- **Title**: always at the very top.
  - Top margin 1.9-3.7% of H; side margins about 1.5%.
  - Spans 97-98% of the width on every one.
- **Main character**: dead centre (head centre x is about 47-53% of W), except WRONG FACTS, which puts the head in the left third.
  - Head diameter is 55-70% of H; head centre about 55% of H.
  - The body is cut off by the bottom edge: only shoulders, shirt and hands show.
- **Side characters**: 1-2 smaller story characters left and right, at 40-50% of the main head size, in the mid-ground.
  - They are often cut by the frame edges.
  - Each holds a topic prop: a machete, a knife, a phone cord, a tank.
- **Timestamp corner** (bottom-right): not kept clear. Props and characters sit under it.

## 2. Typography

- **Words**: 1-2 words, ALL CAPS, a category label rather than the title.
  - Often an adjective + noun: CRAZY PLACES, WRONG FACTS, DARK PAST, LOST HISTORY, WORST DEALS.
  - Or a single word: UNKNOWN.
- **Font**: ultra-wide heavy grotesque (extended, weight 900) with tight spacing. Our Archivo Expanded Black is a near match.
- **Cap height**: not fixed. The words are scaled to fill 97% of the width.
  - About 12 letters: 11.5-12.2% of H.
  - 8-9 letters: 15.6%.
  - 7 letters: 17.2%.
  - 16 letters: about 9%.
- **Two colour treatments**:
  - **White text with a soft dark shadow/halo** (no hard stroke) on colourful or dark scenes: CRAZY PLACES, RABBIT HOLES, DARK PAST, UNKNOWN.
  - **Black text on a white band** that fades into the picture: WRONG FACTS, ANCIENT MYSTERIES, LOST HISTORY, DREAM MEANINGS.
- **Ghost-text layering (the signature trick)**: background → full text → character → the same text again at about 40-45% opacity.
  - The hat or cap overlaps 30-50% of the letter height.
  - The covered letters still read as translucent shapes over it. This gives depth while staying legible.

## 3. Main character treatment (thumbnail-grade art, more detailed than the videos)

- **Outlines**: the same round white head with a thick black outline (about 3% of head diameter); no grey rim.
- **Volume shading**: grey shading inside the head (white centre, grey edges and chin).
- **Expression is always extreme**:
  - Four of seven are screams, with a huge open mouth.
  - The rest are shock, an evil grin, or puzzled.
- **Face detail kit**:
  - Small black oval eyes with heavy drooping upper lids and eye-bags.
  - A huge dark mouth with a pink tongue.
  - Nasolabial cheek folds, chin creases and stubble dots.
  - Light-blue sweat drops.
  - Damage when the topic calls for it: bruises, scratches, grime, blood splatter.
- **Pose**: hands pressed on the cheeks or head (the "Scream" or "Home Alone" pose), or holding the topic prop (a torch, a crumbling tablet).
- **Outfit**: changes to fit the topic. For example, an explorer fedora and satchel for history and mystery topics.

## 4. Backgrounds and storytelling

- **Background**: a full illustrated scene of the topic, not a photo.
  - Examples: a Chernobyl-style plant, a nuclear sign, hills; a conspiracy cork board with red strings; a burning temple versus a boat at sea; a dark night street.
- **Photos**: only appear inside frames (Windows-XP pop-ups in RABBIT HOLES).
- **Split worlds**: DARK PAST splits rainbow versus a dark factory; LOST HISTORY splits fire versus sea. The centre character sits on the split.
- **Small story details** reward a second look: the Porsche crest, the Target logo, "EVERY LIE!!", the eagle flag.

## 5. Colour and contrast

- **Saturation**: mean 19-45%.
  - Bright scenes use blue sky, green hills, and red accents from the mascot's cap and shirt.
  - Dark topics use navy or brown night scenes lit by one warm source.
- **Black ink**: covers 20-32% of the pixels. Heavy outlines everywhere give the "sticker" look that reads at 168 px.
- **Pure white**: covers 10-27% of the pixels (title and head). The big white head is the brightest object and the focal point.

## 6. Title versus thumbnail

The thumbnail text never repeats the title. It names the category, and the title gives the specifics:

| Thumbnail | Title |
|---|---|
| CRAZY PLACES | Every Forbidden Place You're Not Allowed to Visit |
| UNKNOWN | Serial Killers You've Never Heard Of |
| DARK PAST | The Evil Origins of Famous Companies |
| LOST HISTORY | Mysterious Gaps In History We Still Can't Explain |

## 7. How we apply it (our own host, not their mascot)

1. **Character**: the detective host plays the mascot role in the same format. We copy the format, not their character.
2. **Text**: one 2-word category label: EPIC DEATHS, INSANE DEATHS, LEGENDARY DEATHS.
3. **Size and position**: a huge centred head with an extreme face, body cut by the frame, and the fedora overlapping the title with ghost text.
4. **Side characters**: two from the video (Harald and Arrichion, Attila and Crassus, Arrichion and Yi's nephew), each with their death detail.
5. **Backgrounds**: illustrated scenes:
   - A sunset battlefield.
   - A night palace with fire.
   - A split world: the Olympic stadium versus a sea battle.

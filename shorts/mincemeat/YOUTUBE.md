## Description
Britain's top secret agent had no training, no clearance… and no pulse. 💀
In 1943 two British intelligence officers dressed a dead man as a Royal Marines officer, chained fake invasion plans to his wrist and let him wash up in Spain, to make Hitler think the Allies would land in Greece and Sardinia instead of Sicily.

He even carried a fake life: a photo of his fiancée "Pam", two love letters, an engagement-ring receipt, theatre ticket stubs and a grumpy overdraft letter from his bank.

It worked. German forces were sent to Greece and Sardinia, and the Allies landed in Sicily in July 1943.
The man was Glyndwr Michael, a homeless Welshman. His grave in Huelva said "Major William Martin" until 1998, when his real name was finally added.

Sources: Ben Macintyre, *Operation Mincemeat* (2010); Ewen Montagu, *The Man Who Never Was* (1953); UK National Archives; Commonwealth War Graves Commission.
